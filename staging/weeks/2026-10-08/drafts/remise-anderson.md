# Club Parent Ships Remise Journal App

Elite FC parent Eric Anderson released Remise, a free fencing journal on the App Store (iOS 17+). Log bouts, lessons, and opponent notes while they are still fresh; import results from fencingtracker.com so head-to-head history travels with you. Foil, epee, and saber. No account, no ads. App Store: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/remise-fencing-journal/id6799173957 — Eric says he is happy to take feedback.

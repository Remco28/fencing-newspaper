# Parent Q&A — New Points & Youth

**Q.** Does the new points system affect my kid’s Youth events?

**A.** No. USA Fencing’s trial points system covers Cadet, Junior, and Division I (Senior) events only. Youth events stay on the existing points tables — this trial does not touch them.

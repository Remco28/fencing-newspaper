## Key claims (requested)
| # | Claim (who) | Status | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| a1 | "Shaking hands is no longer part of the rules — hasn't been for years" (reply to a foil fencer) | **Verified as of Oct 1, 2026; "for years" contradicted for USA Fencing** | RULES t.122: salute before and at end of bout; no handshake. CALL: t.122 handshake dropped effective 1 Oct 2026 ("no blade tap required either"; FIE has used a no-handshake form "for several years"). RULES-2025 t.122 still required "shake hands with their opponent". |
| a2 | After the bout, salute, then tap blades or shake hands (epee fencer); shake hands or high-five after (foil fencer) | **Contradicted as a rule / OK as optional custom** | RULES t.122 requires only the salute. CALL: "Nothing prevents fencers from shaking hands anyway"; no blade tap required. |
| a3 | Salute opponent and referee; "some referees" want spectators saluted too (epee fencer) | **Partly contradicted** | RULES t.122: salute to opponent, Referee *and spectators* is required before and after every bout. Refusal = 4th-group offense, black card (PENALTY; RULES t.170). |
| a4 | "3-point salute depending on where you are" (foil fencer) | **Contradicted on "depending"** | The three-way salute is the national rule (RULES t.122), not regional. |
| b | "Once you sign the score sheet, THAT'S PERMANENT. Scores cannot be changed once entered" (epee fencer) | **Partly verified (overstated)** | OPS p.18: competitor must sign the pool/DE scoresheet to show they reviewed and agree. Protests allowed during the review period (15 min national / 10 regional / 5 local); recording errors corrected immediately; without consensus "the score recorded on the scoresheet will stand"; "No scores can be changed after the expiration of the review period." Not literally permanent at signing. |
| c | Someone carded for wrong-handed pants in CA; "cracking down on that this season in the Northeast" (foil fencer) | **Unverified (rumor)** | RULES m.25 (knickers fastened below the knee, clothing fully done up, no openings that catch a point) has no handedness rule for breeches. No official source for a Northeast crackdown found. Do not print, or print only as rumor. |
| d | Stopping to look back at the box when you think you hit "is yellow card if no light was on" (commenter, no flair) | **Partly verified** | RULES t.23.4: if you stop before "Halt" and are touched, the touch is valid. RULES t.27.2 + t.170: turning one's back on the opponent is a 1st-group offense (yellow on first offense, touch by offender annulled). Whether a glance back counts is the referee's call; no rule ties it to "no light". Printed only the verified half. |
| e | "First number called is the fencer on the right, except when there's a lefty, they are usually on the left" (epee fencer) | **Verified** | RULES t.22.1: first called stands on the Referee's right, except in a right- vs left-hander bout where the left-hander is called first. |

**Caption** (35 words)
Austria’s Roland Losert receives a book at a reception for Olympians in Freiburg, photographed on 8 November 1964. He had already won both junior and senior world epee titles in 1963, while still a teenager.

**Blurb** (102 words)
The prize in this photograph is a book rather than a medal. Willy Pragher photographed Roland Losert at Freiburg’s reception for Olympians on 8 November 1964. The Austrian had made his breakthrough the previous year, winning both the junior and senior world epee championships in 1963 at eighteen. His career continued across three Olympics: Tokyo 1964, Mexico City 1968 and Munich 1972. Fencing also ran in the family; his father Josef and sister Ingrid were successful competitors. This quiet reception photograph offers a different view of sporting success: a young champion being welcomed home, away from the strip and the medal ceremony.

**Alt text:** Black-and-white photograph of a young man in glasses and a suit receiving a book from a man at left, while another suited man waits behind them beside potted trees.

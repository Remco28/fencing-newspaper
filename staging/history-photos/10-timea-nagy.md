**Caption** (41 words)
Hungary’s Tímea Nagy won Olympic individual epee gold in Sydney in 2000 and defended her title in Athens in 2004. After reversing a retirement announcement, she became individual world champion in 2006. This portrait comes from a 2013 ceremony in Budapest.

**Blurb** (101 words)
Tímea Nagy met Laura Flessel at two decisive stages of her Olympic career. In Sydney in 2000, she edged the reigning champion 15–14 in the semifinal before beating Switzerland’s Gianna Hablützel-Bürki 15–11 for gold. Four years later, Nagy and Flessel faced each other in the Athens final; Nagy won 15–10 to retain the individual epee title. The Hungarian announced her retirement after Athens, then changed her mind and won the 2006 individual world championship. Her eventual retirement came in 2010. The photograph is a later portrait, taken at the women’s team epee award ceremony at the 2013 World Championships in Budapest.

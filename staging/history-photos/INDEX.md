# Photo from History: reusable pile

Epee photos with ready captions, blurbs, credits and licenses. The pile is shared across issues, not tied to one week. Each `NN-slug.md` sits next to its `NN-slug.jpg`, resized to ≤1600 px on the long side, JPEG q82, EXIF stripped, still in color. Grayscale is decided at compose. Distilled 2026-10-18 from Frank's research library (01–12) and his photo agent's second batch (13–18, backup content); the full research and evidence folders stay on Frank's machine.

**Print-ready:** "yes" means at least 730 px wide (2 columns, about 3.3 in at 220 dpi). "1-col only" means at least 355 px wide.

**Rights, in brief:**
1. Paste each entry's credit exactly. Keep the exact license version and URL (2.5 stays 2.5 and 3.0 stays 3.0; never round up to "4.0". Only 14 is genuinely CC BY 4.0).
2. CC BY and BY-SA images need a change notice in the credit if cropped or converted to grayscale ("Cropped and converted to grayscale for this issue."). BY-SA adaptations stay under the same license. Public-domain claims are US-only, as stated in each entry.
3. A license covers copyright only. Never imply that a pictured athlete or photographer endorses Elite FC. After an issue runs, fill in "Used in issue" here.

**Caption** (35 words)
Estonia’s Nikolai Novosjolov on the podium at the 2012 Masters à l’épée. He had won the individual world title in 2010 and would win it again in 2013; this portrait falls between those two championships.

**Blurb** (98 words)
Nikolai Novosjolov’s world titles came three years apart: Paris in 2010 and Budapest in 2013. This portrait was taken between them, at the Masters à l’épée podium on 25 November 2012. The Estonian’s record was broader than those two individual victories. He shared team world silver in 2001 and returned to the individual world podium with silver in 2017. He also competed at four Olympics, from Sydney 2000 through Rio 2016, missing the 2004 Games. The photograph provides a face for that long career, while keeping the event straight: it is a Masters portrait, not either world-title celebration.

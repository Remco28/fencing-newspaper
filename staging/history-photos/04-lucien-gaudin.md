**Caption** (47 words)
Lucien Gaudin, in fencing clothes beside Count Clary, appears in the Amsterdam Olympic report as the individual epee and foil winner. His 1928 epee route included an unusual “extra-final”: after pools, the top four fenced for medals in bouts to ten touches, with a two-touch winning margin.

**Blurb** (121 words)
A round called the final was not necessarily the end at the 1928 Olympic epee competition. After progressing through pools, the top four fencers had to contest an additional medal stage called the “Extra-Final,” with bouts to ten touches and a two-touch winning margin. Lucien Gaudin emerged with gold, beating fellow Frenchman Géo Buchard in the medal decider. He had already shared France’s team epee gold at Paris 1924 and won the 1921 European epee title. The photograph instead shows Gaudin beside Count Clary in the Amsterdam official report, whose caption identifies his individual epee and foil victories.

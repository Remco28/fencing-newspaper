**Blurb** (101 words)
This photograph gives the eye plenty to follow: Fabian Kauter drives forward from the right while Diego Confalonieri answers from the left, their blades bending between them. Marie-Lan Nguyen captured the exchange in the 2012 Trophée Monal final in Paris. Kauter’s epee background was a family affair: his father Christian was a two-time Olympic medalist, and his brother Michael also fenced epee at the Olympics. Fabian earned individual world bronze in both 2011 and 2013. Here, however, the attraction is the action itself.

**Blurb** (101 words)
Before the speed and noise of an epee bout comes a quieter moment. Jörg Fiedler and Bohdan Nikishyn face one another with their weapons raised at the 2013 Masters à l’épée, before their quarterfinal on 1 December. Fiedler was already an Olympic team bronze medalist from Athens 2004, and had won individual European titles in 2011 and 2013. Those achievements form the background to a scene recognizable at any club: two opponents acknowledging each other before competing. The photograph keeps both fencers visible and shows their different ways of holding their masks. No score or result can be read from it.

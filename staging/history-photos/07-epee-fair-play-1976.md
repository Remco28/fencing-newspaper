# 07 · 1976: The light came on—but there was no touch

**Caption** (38 words)
At Montreal in 1976, Boris Onishchenko was disqualified from modern pentathlon after officials found that his epee could register touches without hitting an opponent. This 2006 photograph shows an ordinary electric epee grip and connector, not Onishchenko’s weapon.

**Blurb** (101 words)
During the epee round of the 1976 Olympic modern pentathlon, British competitors challenged touches credited to Boris Onishchenko. In his bout with Jim Fox, the scoring light registered a hit even though his weapon had not reached its target. Examination revealed deliberate tampering that allowed the epee to signal a touch without contact. Onishchenko was disqualified, ending the Soviet team’s medal chances, although his teammates could continue individually. Britain went on to win team gold. The incident occurred in modern pentathlon, not the separate Olympic fencing tournament. The accompanying 2006 equipment photograph shows a normal electric epee, not the weapon involved.

**Caption** (45 words)
Johan Harmenberg displays his medal at the Moscow Olympics in 1980. The Swedish epee fencer had won individual and team world titles on his 1977 world championship debut in the weapon. He competed for another year after Olympic gold, then retired to pursue medical pharmacology.

**Blurb** (111 words)
Johan Harmenberg’s route to Olympic epee gold included a close escape. At Moscow in 1980, he recovered from 7–9 down against Bellone in direct elimination, according to the Swedish Olympic Committee. He then won four of his five bouts in the six-man final pool to take the individual title. Three years earlier, his world championship debut in epee had brought two gold medals in Buenos Aires: individual and team. Moscow produced a different pair of results, with individual gold for Harmenberg and fifth place for Sweden’s team. The medal portrait comes from those 1980 Games. He competed for another year after Moscow, then retired to pursue medical pharmacology, specifically viral immunology.

**Caption** (40 words)
Nathalie Moellhausen became Brazil’s first individual epee world champion in 2019. In a 2020 interview, she described dance among her training activities and discussed her work creating fencing performances. This portrait comes from an earlier fencing flash mob in Paris.

**Blurb** (109 words)
Nathalie Moellhausen’s work has included fencing performances as well as world championship bouts. The 2019 women’s individual epee world champion for Brazil had previously represented Italy. In a June 2020 interview, she described adapting her training during the pandemic with flexibility work, dance, stretching and running. She also discussed developing fencing shows and serving as art director at the 2010 World Championships in Paris. Her account of competition focused on breathing and concentration, including her decision to pursue the last touch of her world-title final despite holding priority in the extra period. The accompanying portrait was taken at a fencing flash mob in Paris, years before her world title.

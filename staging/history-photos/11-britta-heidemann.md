# 11 · Britta Heidemann: a golden triple, with a Beijing connection

- **Photo date:** 2013-08-08
- **What it shows:** Britta Heidemann holding her bronze medal at the women’s individual epee victory ceremony of the 2013 World Championships, Syma Hall, Budapest; the file’s Date field and camera metadata say 8 August 2013.

**Caption** (40 words)
Britta Heidemann holds her individual epee bronze medal from the 2013 World Championships in Budapest. Earlier, she had completed a “golden triple”: world champion in 2007, Olympic champion in 2008 and European champion in 2009, holding all three titles simultaneously.

**Blurb** (105 words)
Britta Heidemann knew Beijing long before she won Olympic individual epee gold there in 2008. At 15, she had spent several months living with a Chinese family in the city; her official biography also describes her fluency in Chinese. Her Beijing victory came between the 2007 world title and the 2009 European title. Her official biography calls her the first woman epee fencer to hold all three crowns simultaneously, the “golden triple.” She also won Olympic team silver in Athens in 2004 and individual silver in London in 2012. The medal in this photograph is her individual bronze from the 2013 World Championships in Budapest.

**Caption** (40 words)
Women’s epee made its Olympic debut in Atlanta in 1996, and Laura Flessel won both available gold medals for France: individual and team. This later portrait shows her at a women’s epee World Cup trophy presentation in Saint-Maur in 2013.

**Blurb** (106 words)
Laura Flessel won the first Olympic women’s individual epee title in Atlanta in 1996, then added team gold with France. The individual final was an all-French contest: she defeated Valérie Barlois 15–12. Born in Guadeloupe, Flessel began fencing at seven and became known as “The Wasp.” Her career stretched across five Olympics, with individual bronze in Sydney in 2000, individual silver and team bronze in Athens in 2004, and appearances in Beijing and London. She also won individual world titles in 1998 and 1999. This portrait was taken at the team trophy presentation of the 2013 Challenge international de Saint-Maur, a women’s epee World Cup event.

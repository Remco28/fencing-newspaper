**Blurb** (117 words)
Paul Anspach’s fencing story has two kinds of achievement: winning, and helping the sport function. In Stockholm in 1912, the Belgian won both individual and team Olympic epee gold. His teammates also made up half of the eight-person individual final. The following year he helped found the Fédération Internationale d’Escrime and became its secretary-treasurer, a post he held until 1920. This full-length portrait is sourced to the Stockholm official report, which labels Anspach the individual epee winner; Commons dates it to July 1912. It is not an image of his Olympic victory. For Elite FC families, Anspach offers an appealing conversation starter: the person who competes can also become the person who builds opportunities for everyone else.

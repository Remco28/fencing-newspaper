Runs

Drag a strip from the bank onto a page to run it; drag a block back to the bank to pull it. Where a block sits is its running order. Drag a photo or comic’s corner to size it (snaps to 1, 2 or 3 columns); drag a story’s bottom edge to grow or shrink it. Some sections switch Wide / Tall on the block. Switch Placement to Manual to freeze this Auto layout and place blocks yourself: grab any block, photos included, and drop it on either page — the dashed outline is where it lands, snapped to the columns and a 0.05-inch grid, and nothing else moves. Drag a block off the page to the left to pull it back to the bank; Esc cancels a drag. A block’s right edge sets its columns and its bottom edge its depth; photos keep their shape and are never cropped. Tap a block or a strip for its slip; the slip’s Page 1 / Page 2 / Bank buttons move it, and the slip has size and Wide / Tall controls. Placement Auto / Manual is in the header. Click anything for its slip. Hatched red on the page is unfilled space; if a page oversets, the overflow hangs in red below the trim line.
